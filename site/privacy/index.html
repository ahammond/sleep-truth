Privacy Policy

Effective November 26, 2026.

The short version. Sleep Truth downloads your data to your device. I never receive it. There is no Sleep Truth server, no account, and no analytics. Your data is 100% yours, and so is the job of keeping it safe.

Who this covers

Sleep Truth is a free tool written by one individual developer (“I”, “me”). It has two parts: a command-line tool that runs on your computer, and a web page that shows your data in your browser. This policy covers both, and this website.

What data is involved

When you sign in, you let the Sleep Truth tool read your sleep records from your device maker's service. That includes sleep periods, sleep stages, times, and time zones. Sleep Truth only asks for the data it needs. You choose what to allow on your device maker's sign-in screen, and you can change it there at any time.

Where your data goes

Your data goes from your device maker's service straight to your computer. It is never sent to me or to any server I run. I do not collect, receive, see, store, use, sell, or share your data. There is no middle man.

The Sleep Truth web page runs entirely in your browser. It reads the file you give it and makes no network requests. Nothing you load into it is uploaded.

How your data is stored

This tool downloads your data to your device. The data stays there until you decide to delete it. That way the tool does not have to download it again to make longer reports. It also gives you a backup of your own data that you control.

On your computer, the tool keeps:

  • your sign-in tokens, readable only by your user account;
  • a local copy of your downloaded sleep records;
  • any files you export, wherever you choose to save them.

You are responsible for the security of your data on your device. That includes your computer, its backups, and any copies you print, export, or share.

Sharing

You decide who sees your data. If you give a printout or an exported file to a doctor or anyone else, that is your choice and your responsibility. I am not involved.

Deleting your data

Run sleep-truth forget. It cancels the tool's access with your device maker, then deletes the sign-in tokens and the downloaded data from your computer. Files you exported yourself are yours to delete. Because I never hold your data, there is nothing for me to delete.

Cancelling access

You can cancel Sleep Truth's access at any time, either with sleep-truth forget or in your device maker's app or account settings. Cancelling takes effect right away.

This website

This website has no cookies, no analytics, and no tracking. Like most websites, it keeps standard access logs: your IP address, the time, the page you asked for, and your browser type. I use them only to keep the site running. They are deleted automatically after 10 days.

Children

Sleep Truth is not meant for children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date. The source of this website is kept under version control.
