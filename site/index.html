Your sleep, on paper, for your doctor.

Sleep Truth downloads your sleep data from your wearable to your own computer. It keeps a local backup there. It prints your nights as a simple timeline, one row per night, so you and your doctor can see the pattern.

There is no middle man. Your data goes straight from your device maker's service to your computer. It never passes through a Sleep Truth server, because there isn't one. The author never sees, receives, or stores your data. It is 100% yours.

How it works

  1. You run the Sleep Truth tool on your computer and sign in to your device maker's service.
  2. The tool downloads your sleep records and keeps them on your computer as a backup.
  3. You export the nights you want and open them in the Sleep Truth page. The page runs in your browser and sends nothing anywhere.
  4. You print the timeline, or save it as a PDF, and share it with whoever you choose.

Your data stays on your device

The downloaded data stays on your computer until you delete it. That way the tool does not have to download it again to make longer reports. You are responsible for keeping your computer and your data secure.

To delete everything the tool downloaded and cut off its access, run sleep-truth forget. Files you exported yourself are yours to delete.
