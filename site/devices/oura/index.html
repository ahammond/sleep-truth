Oura Ring setup

Sleep Truth reads your data through Oura's API. Oura needs an "application" to sign in to. You register your own, so your sign-in goes straight from your computer to Oura with nobody in between.

1. Register an application

Sign in at developer.ouraring.com/applications with your Oura account and create a new application:

  • Display Name: anything you like, for example Sleep Truth.
  • Description: for example Personal sleep printouts.
  • Contact Email: your email address.
  • Website: https://sleep-truth.agh1973.com
  • Privacy Policy: https://sleep-truth.agh1973.com/privacy/
  • Terms of Service: https://sleep-truth.agh1973.com/terms/
  • Redirect URIs: http://localhost:8765/callback
  • Scopes: tick daily and mark it Required. Sleep Truth needs nothing else today.

Read and accept the Oura API Agreement. As the owner of your own application, you are the one agreeing to it.

2. Save the keys

Oura shows a Client ID and a Client Secret. Run this, then paste each one when it asks:

sleep-truth setup

It saves them in ~/.config/sleep-truth/config.json, readable only by your user account. Run it again to change them.

3. Carry on

Go back to Get started, step 4, and sign in.

What Sleep Truth reads

Oura's sleep records. Sleep Truth saves each record as Oura sends it and prints these parts: start and end times with your time zone, sleep stages, movement, and Oura's asleep and in-bed totals. Stages are in 30-second steps, or 5-minute steps on nights where Oura has nothing finer. Naps are included.

Sleep Truth shows data recorded by a consumer wearable. It is not a medical device, not a diagnostic tool, and not medical advice.
